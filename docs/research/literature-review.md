## Proposed research positioning

Test a shared mechanism engine that composes applicability conditions across a configured product, records unresolved branches, and forward-checks backward interventions. Evaluate whether it makes fewer unsupported conclusions without simply refusing all difficult cases. Include an expert-authored numerical workflow to determine whether the benefit comes from the graph or merely from using correct equations.

The decisive comparison with R09 must inspect its full methods against our implemented behavior. If it already provides equivalent condition propagation, refusal, and inverse checking, narrow the contribution to a rigorously evaluated benchmark or another verified algorithmic difference. Do not claim novelty by renaming similar behavior.
