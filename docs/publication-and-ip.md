# Publication and intellectual-property plan

Date: 12 October 2026. Research planning guidance, not a legal opinion or acceptance prediction.

## Choose contribution before venue

IEEE is a publisher and professional organization, not a quality rank. Open access describes reader access and publishing arrangements; indexing describes database inclusion. Neither guarantees quality or acceptance. The phrase “open index journals” is ambiguous, so this plan interprets the goal as a credible peer-reviewed venue with suitable indexing and optional open access.

There is no submission-ready paper yet. First establish a specific method and credible comparative results. A high-quality engineering-informatics study needs evidence beyond a polished prototype.

## Paper structure after experiments

1. Problem and precise engineering-analysis task.
2. Closest prior work, including engineering change and equation-centric graphs.
3. Data contract and proposed condition-composition/inverse method.
4. Independently annotated scenarios and leakage controls.
5. Matched baselines, ablations, transfer effort, and uncertainty.
6. Failure analysis, coverage limits, expert/user evidence, and reproducibility.

Publish numerical results only after running and preserving the experiment. A historical demonstration belongs in a case-study section unless genuinely time-restricted forecasting has been tested.

IEEE Access's current submission guidance asks for disclosure of AI-generated article text. Recheck the selected venue's policy and describe assistance truthfully when preparing the actual manuscript. [Submission guidance](https://ieeeaccess.ieee.org/authors/submission-guidelines/)

## Patent route: two different questions

The engine's method might be considered for an invention disclosure if it develops a concrete new technical mechanism. Separately, an engineering option suggested by the system might become an invention only after its own technical validation and prior-art assessment. Neither follows from the other.

In India, section 3(k) excludes mathematical or business methods, a computer programme per se, and algorithms. The official 2025 CRI guidelines discuss examination of computer-related inventions. An AI label, a knowledge graph, or running on ordinary hardware does not by itself settle eligibility. A qualified patent professional must assess the actual claims and technical contribution. [Act section 3](https://www.ipindia.gov.in/acts/patent-act-1970/section-3), [official CRI guidelines](https://ipindia.gov.in/frontend/pdf/patents/guidelines/GUIDELINES%20FOR%20EXAMINATION%20OF%20COMPUTER%20RELATED%20INVENTIONS%20%28CRIs%29%20-%202025.pdf)

Public disclosure before filing can damage patent novelty; grace periods differ by jurisdiction. Keep the repository private while the team and institution assess the route. Privacy does not reverse any earlier disclosure. [WIPO patent FAQ](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/patents/faq_patents)

## Invention-disclosure worksheet

Record the specific problem, implemented method, difference from the closest paper and patent claims, reproducible technical effect, experimental evidence, known limitations, contributor/inventor roles, institutional ownership, disclosure history, and proposed claim elements. Do not draft a generic “AI knowledge graph innovation system” claim as if it were sufficient.

Use the two patent leads in the literature review for an initial claim-element chart. Expand through cited families, classifications, jurisdictions, and official records with an IP professional. This pass does not establish patentability or freedom to operate.

## Recommended sequence

Complete the method and evidence first. Review ownership and invention disclosure with the guide/institution. If a patent route is justified, resolve filing and disclosure timing with qualified advice before a public repository or preprint. Then prepare the research submission with honest novelty claims and a reproducible package.
